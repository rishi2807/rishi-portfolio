Phoenix: Venue Sourcing Onboarding Redesign

Lead Content Designer · Pendo · 2024

Phoenix was a full rebuild of Cvent's venue sourcing experience, new UI, new features, five different user types migrating from different starting points. I designed five separate onboarding tracks in Pendo, covering everything from first-time free planners to enterprise users converting from the legacy product. Zero developer dependency, shipped before launch.

The problem

Phoenix was a full redesign of Cvent's venue sourcing experience. More features, a different UI structure, and five completely different user types, each migrating from a different starting point.

Five different users, five different approaches

  • First-time free planners: full walkthrough from scratch.
  • Existing free planners (migrated): quick-start tooltips showing what moved where.
  • New enterprise planners: feature overviews based on access level.
  • Existing enterprise planners (Phoenix default): migration-focused orientation.
  • Existing enterprise planners (Phoenix not default): conversion-focused nudges toward adopting Phoenix.

How I built it

All onboarding ran through Pendo, no engineering tickets. Interactive tooltips, step-by-step walkthroughs, and in-app training videos at key decision points.

Content covered the full sourcing journey: RFP creation, venue shortlisting, bid comparison, post-award contract management, and budget tracking.

Impact

  • 5 persona segments served
  • 0 developer dependency
  • Increased feature adoption
