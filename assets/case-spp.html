SPP Reporting: Financial Terminology for Pharma

Lead Content Designer · Compliance reporting · 2024

Pharma event managers at Cvent needed to track healthcare professional spend for regulatory compliance, but inconsistent terminology made the feature unreliable. I standardised SPP language across every label, config screen, and help text, and clarified two separate admin workflows that were being confused with each other. The work directly supported Cvent's 2024 customer-centricity goal.

The context

Pharma event managers track healthcare professional (HCP) expenses across events for regulatory compliance. SPP (Spend Per Person) was unreliable because of inconsistent labels, unclear admin setup, and no cross-event reporting.

What I did

  • Standardised “Spend Per Person” and “SPP” across labels, descriptions, headers, and config screens.
  • Clarified two distinct workflows on the setup page to reduce configuration errors.
  • Created guidance so pharma clients could self-serve answers and reduce support load.

The lesson

In regulated industries, inconsistent terminology creates compliance risk. Aligning language supported Cvent's 2024 customer-centricity goal and made the product reliable for the users who depend on it.
