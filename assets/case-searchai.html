Search AI: full case study

Lead Content Designer · Dashboard, landing, email, content system · 2024–2025

Search AI is a Birdeye product that tracks how businesses appear in AI-generated answers from ChatGPT, Gemini, and Perplexity — a product category with no established UX language when it launched. I owned the full content layer: dashboard copy, landing page, email sequences, and a Brand vs Location language system that kept the product coherent across every surface. The product reached $1M quarterly revenue within three months of launch.

The problem

Search AI tracks how brands show up in AI answers (ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity). The category was new; language needed to feel unified across dashboards, landing pages, emails, and brand vs. location contexts.

What I owned

  • Dashboard and reporting language
  • Landing and go-to-market messaging
  • Email sequences
  • Brand vs. location language system to keep the category coherent

Outcome

  • $1M+ post-launch revenue cited
  • 3 months from beta to launch coverage
  • Employee of the Month; Search AI cited
