Cross-Team Product Workshops

Content Design + Localization · 2023

Writers were writing for products they'd never used end-to-end. Localization teams were translating strings without understanding the workflows they lived in. I designed a repeatable workshop format combining product walkthroughs with open feedback sessions, giving both teams the shared context that sped up sprints and reduced late-stage rework.

The problem

Writers were writing for products they'd never clicked through end-to-end. Localization teams were translating strings without understanding the workflow they lived in. Quality suffered because context was missing.

The format

  • 10-15 min: Workflow overview, what it does, who uses it, what matters.
  • 30 min: Hands-on walkthrough of the actual product, not slides.
  • 15 min: Open feedback on what’s unclear in the flow and copy.

What it changed

  • Writers made fewer assumptions about how features actually work.
  • Localization teams could flag translation risks earlier.
  • Both teams built shared context that sped up later sprints.
